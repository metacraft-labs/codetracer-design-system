## How to choose a colour

1. Only use `colors/ui/*` and `colors/editor/*` (this page). `alias` and `brand` are
   building blocks: never use a ramp step such as `colors-blue-500` in a component.
2. In the app, a token `colors/ui/text/primary/body` is the stylus variable
   `colors-ui-text-primary-body`. It already switches between Dark and Light.
3. Pick the **group** by what you are painting (surface, text, icon, border), then the
   **ramp** by the ground underneath, then the **step** by state.
4. Steps: `default` / `primary` is the resting step, `-hover` its hover, `secondary` and
   `tertiary` are one and two steps further from the ground, `subtle` is quieter,
   `disabled` is the disabled state. A disabled control keeps its fill (D-20).
5. Nothing fits? Don't write a hex value and don't borrow a status colour. Ask Nikolay.
